Walk left to right. KSA holds six preferences for the customer. Cooklist asked for three and was approved for two; it may also update them, so when the customer tells Cooklist a new organic brand, KSA sees the change. Meal Planner asked for three and got two, read-only. Health data like allergies can be shared for reading, but no other agent can ever write it. Anything not approved simply doesn't exist from that agent's point of view.
