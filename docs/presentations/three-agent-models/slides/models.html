The three models

Same platform, three shapes of memory

KSA

Private

Keeps its own: yes — store, brand, milk, delivery, diet, allergies

Reads others: no

Shares: only what its owner approves

Cooklist

Hybrid

Keeps its own: yes — prep time, cuisine, skill

Reads others: selected KSA preferences

Writes others: only if approved (e.g. organic brand)

Meal Planner

Consumer only

Keeps its own: nothing

Reads others: approved preferences only

Writes: never — it can't save anything

KSA is the shopping assistant: it owns the customer's shopping preferences and keeps them private. Cooklist owns cooking preferences and borrows a few from KSA. Meal Planner owns nothing; it only reads what KSA's owner allowed. All three run on the same platform. The difference is only in what they declare and what they request.
