Sensitivity

Each preference is classified, and the platform applies the rules

ClassificationExampleWhat the platform does
Non-sensitiveOrganic brand, storeSaved from what the customer says or the agent infers. Kept up to 3 years.
SensitiveReligion, pregnancySaved only when the customer states it, never inferred. Kept up to 2 years.
RestrictedSSN, card, weaponsNever stored. Blocked even if the agent tries.
+ Health dataAllergiesAsks the customer to confirm, records consent, refused for another adult, never written by another agent.

The content is also scanned: a phone number or SSN typed into any field is blocked, whatever the field's classification.

Owners classify each preference when they create it. The platform then does the rest: sensitive values must come from the customer, restricted content never gets stored, and health data has its own confirmation and consent flow. Worth saying out loud: the Privacy Standard has four tiers and a restricted tier that can be stored with Privacy Office approval; mapping that onto these three levels is an open item.
